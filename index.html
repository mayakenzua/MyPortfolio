FAQ

01 What's your typical project timeline?

It depends on scope. A simple brand identity can take 3–4 weeks, while a full web or app project typically runs 6–12 weeks.

02 How do you structure your pricing?

Project-based or hourly, depending on the work. Most clients prefer fixed rates for predictable budgets. A 50% deposit starts the work; the rest is due on completion.

03 What's included in your design process?

Discovery and strategy, research and concepts, design exploration, revisions from your feedback, and final delivery of all assets.

04 Do you work with clients remotely?

Absolutely. I work with clients worldwide using video calls for key meetings and collaborative design tools for feedback.

05 How many revisions are included?

Most projects include 2–3 rounds of revisions. Consolidated feedback makes each round count.
